Monsters

Watcher · floating eye. It lines up with the robot, its mouth glows, then it spits a fireball. Takes three hits.
Seeker · burning skull. Fast: it charges in and bites. One hit is enough.

They sleep until they see the robot or hear a gunshot, then hunt it down. Shots and fireballs fly straight along the rows and columns of the maze: to hit, you have to line up.

How it works

  1. Sensors to text. The game describes what the robot perceives: free cells in each direction, the way to the nearest unexplored area, the crosshair, and for a monster off the line of fire, the move that lines it up.
  2. One forward pass. Qwen3.5 runs on your GPU through WebGPU. The system prompt and worked examples are computed once and cached.
  3. Read, don't generate. Jev-like, we read the probability of four words: Forward, Left, Right, Shoot. The situation and its mirror image are averaged.
  4. Act. The most likely action wins; physically impossible moves are masked. Keyboard: ↑←→ Space.
